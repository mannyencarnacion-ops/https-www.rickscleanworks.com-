Our story

We love to clean

We know that not everyone does — but we’re pretty sure everyone loves a clean space. That’s why we’ve made it our full-time job to help people live and work in cleaner spaces.

At Rick’s Clean Works, we take pride in creating clean, comfortable spaces for homes and businesses. We focus on quality work, attention to detail, and dependable service you can count on. Whether it’s routine cleaning or a deep clean, our goal is simple — leave every space looking its absolute best.

We’ll come to your home or business with a smile and a sponge, and we won’t leave until your space is sparkling. We treat every space we clean as though it were our own — with respect and integrity.
